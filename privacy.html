Privacy policy

Swipewise keeps your data on your phone, on your own computer and in your own Google Drive. It has no servers, and it sends your data to no one.

Last updated: 24 October 2026.

What Swipewise reads

  • On your phone: bank SMS about card and savings-account payments, bills and statements. Other messages are read only to decide that they are not bank payments, and are not stored.
  • On your computer: the bank statements you choose to fetch, and the rules files you maintain.

How Google user data is accessed and used

  • Swipewise requests one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It lets Swipewise create, read and delete only the files Swipewise itself created in your Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Those files hold your payment records, your category choices, your card rules and your statement bill amounts. They are used only to keep the phone app and the computer program in step with each other.
  • Swipewise's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored only on your phone, on your computer and in the "Swipewise sync" folder of your Google Drive.
  • Swipewise does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train any model.
  • Nobody else, including the developer, can see your data.

Removing your data and access

  • Delete the "Swipewise sync" folder from your Google Drive.
  • Remove Swipewise's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstall the app to delete everything it stored on your phone.
