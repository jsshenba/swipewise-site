Swipewise

A personal money tracker for credit cards and savings accounts in India. It reads bank SMS on your phone, shows which card would have earned more, tracks reward caps and bill due dates, and checks SMS payments against your bank statements.

What it does

  • Reads card and savings-account payment SMS on your Android phone as they arrive.
  • Works out the rewards each payment earned, and alerts you when another card would have earned more.
  • Tracks reward caps, milestone offers and monthly spending against a budget.
  • Groups card bills by the month you pay them and reminds you before each due date.
  • Pairs with a companion program on your own computer that reads your bank statements and matches them against the phone's records.

How Google Drive is used

The phone app and the computer program exchange data through one folder in your own Google Drive, named "Swipewise sync". Swipewise asks only for Google's narrowest Drive permission, which lets it see the files it created and nothing else in your Drive.

Privacy policy

Swipewise is a personal project, not a bank or a financial service.
